What Contributes to the Niche Scores?

The niche scores are influenced by various factors. From the histogram, it shows the certain neighborhoods the 6 Train passes through frequently, which may affect its accessibility. As for the 2 Train, these factors can vary depending on the average household income, which will determine if it's a good place for families to live.
